# How-to videos — Resuming

Scripts and filming notes for the YouTube how-to series. The launch playbook is [marketing.md](./marketing.md). Ideas we have not scripted yet stay in the chat until they land here.

**Rules (same as every public post)**

- Show only what ships. No medicines, widgets, circles, or admin screens.
- No health claims. Breathing and vitals are “log it / follow along,” never what it cures.
- No streak shame. A missed day is just a day.
- Real app, English UI for this file. Hindi scripts get their own section later.
- Free, no ads, data not sold. Play link in the description, never an APK.
- Never add `utm_campaign` to a Play URL. YouTube long-form uses `utm_source=youtube` and `utm_medium=video`.

### What to film on

Use a real Android phone, English, a fresh guest run for scenes 1–6. Film scene 7 (welcome back) from a **prepared** account that has been quiet for a few days, so the card is real.

**Demo picks (stick to these so cuts match):**

| Setup question | Answer | What you add |
|----------------|--------|----------------|
| Regular workout? | Yes | Walking |
| A little pranayam every day? | Yes | Anuloma Viloma only |
| Community days? | No | — |
| Dates to remember? | Yes | `Electricity bill`, kind: Bill or tax, day: Today |
| Daily vitals? | Yes | Water only |
| How small? | Keep the small option | Walking 5 minutes if asked |
| Nudge time? | One evening time, then Continue | e.g. 7:00 pm |

Skip Explore more. Skip creating a custom habit. Skip weight, BP, heart rate, steps, protein, fasting. Skip medicines (they should not appear on Android).

**Prepared account for “missed a day”:** Walking has a few logged days, then 3–5 quiet days, so Today shows the welcome-back card: “It’s been a few days. Welcome back.” and a button like **Start 5 minutes**.

Record portrait on the phone, then place the recording in a 16:9 cream frame to match the site. Captions: large, two lines, upper third so the nav is visible.

### Full voice-over (read this)

If you’ve ever missed a day of a new habit, you know the feeling. Most apps treat it like you broke something. Resuming doesn’t. There is no streak to lose.

In the next five minutes I’ll show you the whole app: how to set up a few small things, tick them off on one list called Today, keep a bill in the same place, and what to do when a day goes quiet.

It’s free, with no ads. On Android, get it on Google Play. On an iPhone or a computer, open resuming.me. No account needed to try it.

Tap Get started.

Setup is a few yes-or-no questions. Two or three yeses are a good start.

I’ll say yes to a regular workout, and pick Walking. Start small. Even ten minutes counts — five is fine too.

Yes to a little pranayam. I’ll pick Anuloma Viloma. A follow-along video plays with the timer. This is not medical advice. It’s just something to do along with.

Any dates to remember? A bill, a doctor visit, a wedding. I’ll add an electricity bill for today. Same taps if yours is rent, or a dentist.

Daily numbers? I’ll add Water. You can add weight or steps later.

If it asks how small to start, keep it small. Two minutes counts. I’ll set one evening nudge, and continue.

This is Today. Your whole day lives here.

The first time, a short guide names the four buttons at the bottom. Today is the list. Activity is where you add or remove habits. Reminders is bills, visits, and shared dates. Health is water, weight, steps. Tap Got it.

Water is a tap. Each tap is a glass. That’s it.

Walking has a Start button. Tap it, and a timer runs. Need to stop? Pause. The minutes you already did are kept.

Open Anuloma Viloma. Tap Start. The video plays inside the habit, and the timer starts with it. Prefer your own teacher? You can paste a YouTube link on any habit later.

The bill sits on the same Today list until it’s done. Pay it when you pay it. It waits.

Now Reminders. This is every date in one place: yours, ones family shared, and community days like festivals.

I’ll add Mom’s birthday, and turn on Every year so it comes back. You can also share an event on WhatsApp so family adds it in one tap. That’s its own video. For now, know that it’s here.

Here’s the part the app is named for.

If a few days go quiet, you don’t get a broken streak. You get a small welcome back. Start with five minutes of walking. That still counts. The days you already showed up are still there.

You can skip a single day on purpose too. The app says it plainly: this day stays quiet, and it isn’t a miss.

Without an account, the list stays on this phone for seven days. To keep it, tap Save your progress and continue with Google.

That’s the whole app. Pick a few small things. Tick them on Today. Missed a day? Just resume.

Links are in the description. Free on Google Play, or try it at resuming.me.

### Edit notes

- **Montage the setup.** If S2–S10 play in real time, the video overruns. Cut to the tap and the next screen. Keep the yes/no headings readable for a beat.
- **Sound.** Taps quietly under the VO. Follow-along video: keep it low or mute, so it does not fight the voice. Music low or off.
- **Anuloma Viloma caption** must include “Not medical advice” while the video is on screen.
- **Do not** show a Google account email, a real home address on a bill photo, or a family phone number.
- **Do not** say the app lowers blood pressure, improves sleep, or treats anything.
- If the tab guide was already dismissed on the filming phone, clear app data or use a fresh guest so it appears.
- US viewers hear “rent, or a dentist” once. India viewers see “Electricity bill.” One English video, both markets.
- A 20-second Short can be cut from 4:08–4:38 (welcome back) with the caption **Missed a day? Just resume.** Upload that separately. Do not replace this video with the Short.
