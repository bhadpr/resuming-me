Delete your data

Resuming by Cheerful Games, Inc.

You can delete one habit and keep your Resuming account, or delete the account and everything stored with it. Both happen in the app.

Delete one habit

  1. Sign in to Resuming with Google or your email link.
  2. Open the habit.
  3. Tap Delete permanently, then Delete forever.

This removes that habit and its log history. Your account, name, email, and other habits stay.

Delete your account and all of your data

  1. Sign in to Resuming with Google or your email link.
  2. Open Settings (gear icon).
  3. Under Account, tap Delete my account.
  4. You can export a copy first. Then type DELETE to confirm.

This permanently removes:

  • Your profile, including the name and email on the account
  • Your habits, metrics, and log history
  • Feedback you sent while signed in

What we keep

Deleted habits and deleted accounts are removed from Resuming. We do not keep a separate copy in the app. Encrypted backups may still hold that data until those backups expire in the ordinary backup cycle. We may also keep a limited record when the law or security requires it. Anonymous usage counts that are not tied to your account can remain.

Need help?

If you cannot access the app, email resuming.me@gmail.com from the address on your account. Cheerful Games, Inc. will verify the request and delete the data.
