About

Resuming by Cheerful Games, Inc.

Start again, two minutes counts.

Miss a few days and Resuming meets you there, with one small way back. Rest, a skip, and a pause are not failures. Insights keeps the record of what you pick back up.

Built for a single person who wants a calm, no-shame tool — not gamification, not social pressure, just a clearer view of what you are (and aren’t) picking back up.
