# New ideas

A parking place for marketing and growth ideas that are not scheduled yet. The launch plan is in [marketing.md](./marketing.md), and the go-live checklist is in [docs/LAUNCH.md](./docs/LAUNCH.md).

How to use this file:

- Add a new idea at the bottom of the list with the next number. Write what it is, why it should work, how big it is, and when to start.
- When you decide to do one, move it into `marketing.md` as a task and mark it "Scheduled" here with the date.
- Every idea keeps the launch rules: nothing unshipped, no health claims, Play link only, no streak shame. Use a tagged link, and never add `utm_campaign` (the app reads `utm_source` plus `utm_campaign` as a referral group code).

Sizes: S is an afternoon, M is a few days, L is a week or more.

## Highest payoff for the effort

### 1. Event organizers as the way in

Every shared event page has "Get it on Google Play", and one organizer reaches 50 to 500 people. This loop is already in the app. This idea pushes it on purpose.

- List ten organizers you know: a Ganesh mandal, a satsang, a garba group, a society committee, a family planning a wedding.
- Offer to set up their next event link yourself. Ten organizers will bring in more installs than a hundred random Reels, and everyone who adds the event has a reason to open the app.
- Tag the Play button on the event page and count installs from shared links on their own line in the weekly table.
- Done when ten organizers have been asked, and installs from shared events are counted apart from other sources.

### 2. Group admins and teachers first

- Message the admin of each group before you post in it. Two lines: what the app is, and whether a short clip is welcome. A "yes" is often a pin. A "no" saves you from a ban.
- Send 15 to 20 yoga and Pranayam teachers, senior walking-group leaders and Heartfulness centre volunteers a two-line message and a one-minute screen recording. Ask for nothing, and offer to answer questions. One teacher who sends it to a class is worth more than an ad.
- Do not suggest the app is endorsed by, or partnered with, any teacher or organization unless it is.
- Done when each admin and teacher has been asked once, with the answer written down.

### 3. One link that picks the store

- A small route on resuming.app: `resuming.app/go/<source>`. Android goes to the Play link tagged with `<source>`. Everyone else goes to `https://resuming.me/start`.
- It gives one short, clean link for a WhatsApp Status, a bio, or a printed card, and it fixes the "Android link sent to an iPhone" problem.
- Done when a test from an Android phone and from an iPhone or desktop each land in the right place, and the Android click shows under its own source in Play Console.

### 4. Invitation cards and QR codes

- Three plain, free templates: a wedding, a puja, and a birthday. Each says "Add to Resuming" and shows the event link, which organizers can reuse.
- A QR code that opens the shared event link, for a printed or digital invitation.
- This is the app's own use case, advertised where the audience already is.
- Done when the three cards exist in English and Hindi, and a QR scanned from a second phone adds the event.

## Content ideas

### 5. Seven-day Resume series

- Seven Reels or Shorts, one a day, with the smallest possible step: "Day 1: one glass of water. Day 2: two minutes of breathing." Ask people to comment "Day 1" and join in.
- It is content, not a promise about outcomes. Every claim stays at "small steps count".
- Done when seven clips are posted in a week, and views and installs per tagged link are recorded.

### 6. Founder diary

- Short, honest Stories and one Reel: "The first 24 hours of my app", with real numbers from Play Console and the admin Analytics screen (installs, one bug, one review).
- People follow one-person builders. It also gives Peerlist and Indie Hackers something real to react to.
- Done when the first-day Reel is posted and the numbers in it match the dashboards.

### 7. Evergreen YouTube timers

- "5 minute Kapalabhati timer, follow along" and "Anulom Vilom for beginners, 10 minute timer". People search these all year, and the footage already exists: the follow-along video inside the habit.
- Keep to how to follow the timer, with no claims about benefits (the same rule as the guides in Phase 14).
- Done when two videos are up with titles and descriptions in English and Hindi, and the tagged link is in the description.

### 8. Shareable cards people repost

- About ten images with one line and the logo: "Missed a day? Just resume.", "A missed day is just a day.", "Two minutes counts."
- People post these to Status without being asked. The Instagram versions add "Tag someone who needs this".
- This is the same set as the image cards in task P15-03 in `marketing.md`. Make them once.
- Done when the cards exist in English and Hindi.

### 9. Regional-language Reels

- The app runs in Gujarati, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil and Bengali. A Reel in the viewer's own language lands harder in a regional group than a Hindi one.
- Post a language only if the poster can read the replies. Have a native speaker check the captions.
- Done when one Gujarati and one Marathi Reel are posted, and a native speaker has checked each.

### 10. Fifteen minutes of comment help a day

- Search for people asking "how do I restart my walking habit" or "which Pranayam timer app" on Instagram, Facebook groups and Quora. Give a real answer first. Mention the app once, and only when it fits.
- It brings fewer people than a Reel, but they are already looking.
- Done when it has run for two weeks, with the installs from these links counted as their own line.

## Seasons the app already fits

### 11. Season calendar

The community calendar in the app already ships Diwali (8 Nov 2026) and Christmas (25 Dec 2026). That is a marketing calendar as well as a feature.

| When | Idea | Clip |
|------|------|------|
| Late October to 8 Nov | Diwali. "Add Diwali to your Today list" | Open the community calendar, tap Add |
| Mid-November onward (check the dates for your region) | Wedding season. Shared invitation links are the most useful feature then | The WhatsApp share clip (B4) with a card photo |
| Late December | Christmas, then the end-of-year "new habits" talk | A carousel of three small things |
| First two weeks of January | New Year resolutions. "Missed January 3rd? Just resume." | A Reel, and the Instagram and Facebook budget that waited |

- Do not state a statistic about how many resolutions fail unless it has a source. "Most of us miss a day" is enough.
- Done when each slot has a Reel and a post ready a week before its date.

## Channels worth a small test

### 12. WhatsApp Channel for Resuming

- Use the broadcast feature, not a group. One short message a week: a restart tip, a festival reminder, a small new feature. People follow without sharing their number.
- Link it from Instagram, Facebook and the site.
- Done when it has run for two weeks, with follower count and installs from its link in the weekly table.

### 13. Instagram Collab posts

- The Collab feature costs nothing and shows the post on both profiles. Start with yoga teachers and walking-group hosts who have a few thousand followers, before any paid Reel.
- Done when three collabs have run, with installs per tagged link recorded.

### 14. ShareChat and Moj

- Large Hindi and regional audiences. The plan already allows them as an option (TikTok is the one that stays out).
- Reuse the same vertical clip without changes, as a free test in week 3.
- Done when two weeks of results are in the weekly table, and you have decided to keep or drop each.

## Process

### 15. Kit for referral group members

Members post for money per five-day return, so what they post is on us (see task P15-15 in `marketing.md`).

- One pack per member: the clips, a caption in their language, the line "free, no ads, not medical advice", and the rules. No fake installs, no asking for reviews, no health claims, no spam in groups that forbid it. Posts that break the rules are not paid.
- Done when each member has the pack with their own link, and any spike flag in `/admin/groups` has a note about what the member posted.

## Small product nudges

These help growth, and they must stay gentle. Build one only behind a real signal (people asking, or a clear drop in the sharing loop). Neither may appear on first open or after an error.

### 16. Invite the family after sharing an event

- After someone shares an event, show "Invite the family to Resuming" with the same link, tagged `in-app-share`. They have just shown they have a group.
- Done when it is built, the tagged installs show in Play Console, and day-1 return for these people is read against the average.

### 17. A shareable "Resumed" card

- After tapping Resume, offer a small card: "I resumed today." It matches the brand and gives people something to share.
- Optional always. If it ever feels like pressure, remove it.
- Done when people have asked for it, it is built, and it never appears unprompted.
