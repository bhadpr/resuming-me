## Full description (max 4000)

Resuming is one simple daily list for your medicines, habits, blood pressure and reminders. And when you miss a day, it helps you start again. No guilt, no broken streaks.

MEDICINES ON TIME
• Add each medicine with its days and times, before or after food
• Get an alarm when it is time, and tick it off when you take it
• Take a photo of the strip or bottle so you never mix them up
• Allopathic, Ayurvedic or Homeopathic

HABITS THAT FIT YOUR DAY
• Walking, pranayam, Heartfulness meditation, prayer, reading and more
• Start small. Two minutes counts.
• Use the built-in timer, or just tick it off
• Missed a few days? Resuming suggests a smaller step to get going again

VITALS IN ONE PLACE
• Note your blood pressure, weight and heart rate in a few seconds
• See how they change over the weeks
• Daily steps from your phone through Health Connect

REMINDERS FOR EVERYTHING ELSE
• Bills, doctor visits, birthdays and festivals
• They wait on your Today list until they are done
• Every-year reminders come back on their own

MADE FOR INDIA
• English, हिन्दी, ગુજરાતી, मराठी, తెలుగు and தமிழ்
• Try it without an account. Sign in with Google or email to keep your data safe.
• Free. No ads. Your data stays yours.

Resuming is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Always follow your doctor's advice about your medicines and health.
