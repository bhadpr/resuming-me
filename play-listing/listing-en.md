## Full description (max 4000)

Resuming is one simple daily list for your habits, vitals and reminders. And when you miss a day, it helps you start again. No guilt, no broken streaks.

HABITS THAT FIT YOUR DAY
• Walking, running, workouts, pranayam, prayer, reading and more
• Start small. Two minutes counts.
• Use the built-in timer, or just tick it off
• Missed a few days? Resuming suggests a smaller step to get going again

VITALS IN ONE PLACE
• Note your blood pressure, weight and heart rate in a few seconds
• See how they change over the weeks
• Daily steps from your phone through Health Connect

REMINDERS FOR EVERYTHING ELSE
• Bills, doctor visits, birthdays and festivals
• They wait on your Today list until they are done
• Every-year reminders come back on their own

MADE FOR INDIA
• English, हिन्दी, ગુજરાતી, मराठी, తెలుగు and தமிழ்
• Try it without an account. Sign in with Google or email to keep your data safe.
• Free. No ads. Your data stays yours.

Resuming is a wellness and habit app. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.
